Rust makes it red

Iron in the red soil has rusted, giving the planet its iconic red color. Its sunsets glow blue.

A volcano taller than Everest

Olympus Mons is about 22 km high, nearly 2.5 times Mount Everest, and the tallest volcano known in the solar system.

A canyon as wide as a continent

Valles Marineris stretches roughly 4,000 km, about the width of the United States.
